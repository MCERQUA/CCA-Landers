# Frequently Asked Questions - Waterproofing Insurance

## What types of insurance do waterproofing contractors need?

Waterproofing contractors need several essential insurance coverages: General Liability Insurance to protect against property damage and bodily injury claims, Professional Liability Insurance for errors and omissions, Workers' Compensation Insurance for employee protection, Commercial Auto Insurance for vehicles and equipment transport, and Pollution Liability Insurance due to the environmental risks associated with waterproofing materials and chemicals.

## How much does waterproofing contractor insurance cost?

Waterproofing contractor insurance costs vary based on business size, location, services offered, and claims history. General liability insurance typically ranges from $800-2,500 annually, while comprehensive packages including all necessary coverages can range from $3,000-8,000 per year. Factors affecting cost include annual revenue, number of employees, types of waterproofing services, and coverage limits selected.

## Does general liability insurance cover waterproofing failures?

General liability insurance may cover property damage resulting from waterproofing failures, but coverage depends on the specific policy terms and circumstances. Professional liability insurance specifically covers errors, omissions, and failures in workmanship. For comprehensive protection against waterproofing failures, contractors should carry both general liability and professional liability coverage.

## What is pollution liability insurance for waterproofing contractors?

Pollution liability insurance protects waterproofing contractors against claims related to contamination from chemicals, sealants, and materials used in waterproofing applications. This coverage is crucial because many waterproofing materials contain chemicals that could potentially cause environmental damage or health issues if improperly handled or applied.

## Are waterproofing contractors required to have workers' compensation insurance?

Workers' compensation requirements vary by state, but most states require waterproofing contractors with employees to carry workers' compensation insurance. This coverage protects employees who suffer work-related injuries or illnesses and protects employers from related lawsuits. Even in states where it's not mandatory, workers' compensation is highly recommended for waterproofing contractors due to the physical nature of the work.

## What does completed operations coverage include for waterproofing work?

Completed operations coverage protects waterproofing contractors against claims that arise after a project is finished. This includes damage from waterproofing failures, leaks that develop months or years later, or other issues related to completed waterproofing work. This coverage is essential because waterproofing problems may not become apparent until long after project completion.

## How does commercial auto insurance work for waterproofing contractors?

Commercial auto insurance covers vehicles used for business purposes, including transporting waterproofing equipment, materials, and workers to job sites. This coverage includes liability protection if a business vehicle causes an accident, as well as physical damage protection for the vehicle itself. It also covers specialized waterproofing equipment and tools while in transit.

## What factors affect waterproofing insurance premiums?

Several factors influence waterproofing insurance premiums: business location and local claim frequency, types of waterproofing services offered (residential vs. commercial), annual revenue and business size, claims history and safety record, employee count and experience levels, coverage limits and deductibles selected, and specific risks like working in confined spaces or with hazardous materials.

## Do I need separate insurance for basement vs. exterior waterproofing?

Most comprehensive waterproofing insurance policies cover both basement and exterior waterproofing services under the same policy. However, specific coverage needs may vary based on the different risks involved. Basement waterproofing might require additional coverage for confined space work, while exterior waterproofing might need enhanced coverage for excavation activities and potential utility damage.

## What should waterproofing contractors look for in an insurance policy?

Waterproofing contractors should seek policies with adequate liability limits (typically $1-2 million per occurrence), pollution liability coverage, completed operations protection, coverage for specialized equipment and tools, business interruption insurance, and flexible terms that accommodate seasonal work patterns. The policy should also include coverage for subcontractors and provide protection for both residential and commercial projects.

## How quickly can I get waterproofing contractor insurance coverage?

Most waterproofing contractor insurance policies can be issued within 24-48 hours of application approval. Emergency or immediate coverage needs can often be accommodated with temporary binders while the full policy is processed. The speed of coverage depends on the completeness of your application, business documentation, and whether additional underwriting review is required.

## Does waterproofing insurance cover damage to existing structures?

Coverage for damage to existing structures depends on your specific policy terms and the circumstances of the damage. General liability insurance typically covers accidental damage you cause to existing structures during waterproofing work. However, damage due to faulty workmanship might be excluded from general liability and covered under professional liability insurance instead.
